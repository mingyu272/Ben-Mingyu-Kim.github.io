ARMOR LAB / Research

Research

Connecting distributed intelligence, uncertainty-aware sensing, and autonomous systems in the field.

Our research develops real-time, probabilistic, and optimization-based algorithms for autonomous systems operating in uncertain and dynamic environments. Three complementary thrusts connect fundamental methods with applications in maritime awareness, environmental monitoring, and resilient infrastructure.

RESEARCH THRUST 01

Multi-Robot Intelligence

Distributed decision-making, coordination, task allocation, and adaptive autonomy for heterogeneous robotic teams.

We study how drones, surface vessels, underwater vehicles, and fixed sensors can collaborate in dynamic, data-limited settings.

  • Cooperative mission planning and distributed control
  • Task allocation and communication for mixed robot teams
  • Decentralized estimation and optimization with time-varying constraints
  • Real-time adaptation for persistent monitoring and environmental exploration

RESEARCH THRUST 02

Uncertainty-Aware Sensing

Probabilistic modeling, sensor placement, anomaly detection, Bayesian inference, and adaptive information acquisition.

We use Poisson processes and log-Gaussian Cox processes (LGCP) to characterize uncertain target arrivals and environmental processes, combining stochastic models with optimization for fixed and mobile sensor networks.

  • Bayesian inference, state estimation, and heterogeneous data fusion
  • Anomaly detection for vessel traffic and coastal environments
  • Void-probability and variance-aware sensor-placement methods
  • Adaptive information acquisition and near-real-time sensor repositioning

RESEARCH THRUST 03

Autonomous Field Systems

UAV, USV, and sensor-network systems for maritime awareness, environmental monitoring, infrastructure inspection, and resilient operations.

We connect planning and inference with autonomous aerial, surface, and sensor-network systems to support persistent observation in complex environments.

  • USV–UAV collaborative sensing and adaptive water-quality sampling
  • Maritime domain awareness and distributed environmental monitoring
  • UAS mission planning for infrastructure inspection and emergency response
  • Digital-twin and data-driven modeling for prediction and decision support
