October 2026

Paper accepted for publication in Sensors

We’re excited to share that our paper, “Toward Near-Optimal and Scalable Placement and Orientation of Directional Sensors for Stochastic Directional Trajectory Detection,” has been accepted for publication in Sensors (MDPI)! This work develops a scalable framework for jointly positioning and orienting directional sensors to detect targets moving along uncertain trajectories. It combines stochastic modeling with efficient optimization to support sensor-network design. Thank you to my co-authors for their contributions and collaboration. We look forward to sharing this work with the sensing, robotics, and control communities! The article is forthcoming and is not yet available online. We’ll add the publication link once it is released.

Congratulations to Md Muhtasim Fuad! His paper, “Gamma-Laplace Surrogate for Variance Aware Sensor Placement for Detecting Poisson Distributed Targets,” has been accepted to the 2026 IEEE Conference on Decision and Control (CDC).
